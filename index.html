      How the tap-to-pay kiosk works

      1. Tap

        A customer taps a card or phone on the kiosk reader.

      2. Approve

        The payment processor approves or declines the charge.

      3. Notify

        On approval, the kiosk sends the wash order to the CleanTrak API.

      4. Open

        CleanTrak opens the gate and the car pulls into the wash.
